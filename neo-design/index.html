One system for everything on Neo's prompt box

Built on shadcn/ui (new-york), with the brand's palette and type feeding its tokens. Containers 12px, controls 32px tall with 8px corners, number badges 6px, chips and tags round. Text is 14 and 12 only: 13 isn't on the brand scale.
